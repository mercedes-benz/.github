## 👋 Hello, Open Source Enthusiasts!

**Welcome to the Mercedes-Benz Group Open Source Platform!** We are delighted to have you here. While our collection of Open Source projects is still growing, we are excited to share what we have been working on and to invite you along on our journey.

---

## ✨ Our Commitment to Being Truly Open

At Mercedes-Benz, we have been redefining our software development culture – moving away from a strict **"we develop everything ourselves"** mindset toward a genuine **embrace of Open Source**. This transformation is guided by our [FOSS Manifesto](https://opensource.mercedes-benz.com/manifesto/).
